--- layout: default css: app --- {%- comment -%} App landing page layout. Chains to default.html. IMPORTANT: this file's own `css: app` above only affects a page that has no `css:` of its own -- Jekyll does NOT cascade a layout's front matter down to the pages that use it. Every page on this layout must still set `css: app` itself (as cloud-storage-backup-drive.md does), or it renders with shell.css only and no pages/app.css. Confirmed the hard way: post.html originally relied on exactly this false cascade and pages/post.css silently never loaded. Fully front-matter-driven; the page body is empty (user, 29 Sep: no pasted guide text -- each app page is its own designed page). Required front matter (STATUS.md section 4): title, description, css (must be "app"), app_name, tagline, ios_url and/or android_url, category, features (icon, title, text), how_it_works (3x {title, text}), faqs (q, a). Optional, each adding a section: moments (+ moments_heading, moments_sub; 4x {icon, title, text}), compare ({heading, without[], with[], optional without_title / with_title}), tips (+ tips_heading; 3x {title, text}), guides (Daily Info slugs). Also steps_heading and trust_text (an app-specific privacy sentence -- only ever something confirmed about that particular app). Everything written for an app must stay inside what's confirmed about it (STATUS.md section 1): moments describe situations, tips and FAQs give general advice; neither may imply a feature the app isn't known to have. The #download id must stay on the hero's store badges. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Schema: applicationCategory is one of schema.org's application types (every app is a utility), not the page's visible category label. The apps are free to download (owner, 3 Oct), so the Offer is price 0; currency is required by Google even for free apps. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _clean_url = page.url -%} {%- comment -%} This page's own apps.yml entry (for its glyph/tone tile), found by URL: /document-reader-pdf -> document-reader-pdf. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _slug = page.url | remove_first: "/" -%} {%- assign _app = site.data.apps | where: "slug", _slug | first -%} {%- assign _short = _app.short | default: page.app_name -%} {%- comment -%} Platforms come from which store links the page actually has -- never hardcoded, since most of the apps are Android-only. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.ios_url and page.android_url -%} {%- assign _os = "iOS, Android" -%}{%- assign _platforms = "iPhone and Android" -%} {%- elsif page.ios_url -%} {%- assign _os = "iOS" -%}{%- assign _platforms = "iPhone" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign _os = "Android" -%}{%- assign _platforms = "Android" -%} {%- endif -%} {% include faq-schema.html faqs=page.faqs %} {%- comment -%} Everything visible sits inside the app's tone scope (shell.css "App tones"): step numbers, card icons and the stage take this app's colour. The header and footer stay outside it, in brand blue. Layout (Phase 3, 20 Oct), an apple.com product page in the homepage's rhythm, white and grey in turn, every heading and lead on the shared scale: a centred opening (the app's name very large, its tagline, the store badges with id="download") over a grey stage with the app on a phone, its features as labels around it, and links to the sections; then the moments as cards (white), how it works as numbered columns (grey), what it does as cards, a bento when there are six or more (white), with and without as a light and a dark card (grey), tips as numbered cards (white), related guides on a shelf (grey), the FAQ (white), the other apps (grey), and a closing pair: get the app, your privacy (white). Every section is named by its heading (aria-labelledby); every icon is inline (icon.html). {%- endcomment %}
