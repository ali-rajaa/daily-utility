IMPORTANT: this file's own `css: app` above only affects a page that has no `css:` of its own -- Jekyll does NOT cascade a layout's front matter down to the pages that use it. Every page on this layout must still set `css: app` itself (as cloud-storage-backup-drive.html does), or it renders with shell.css only and no pages/app.css. Confirmed the hard way: post.html originally relied on exactly this false cascade and pages/post.css silently never loaded. Structured parts come from front matter; the page's markdown body is a long-form guide (user, 29 Sep: about 2,500 visible words per app page), written separately for each app so no two pages share body text. Optional: guide_heading and guide_sub (the guide section's H2 and intro), steps_heading (the how-it-works H2), trust_text (an app-specific privacy sentence for the trust band -- only ever something confirmed about that particular app).
