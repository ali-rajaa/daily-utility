--- layout: default css: app --- {%- comment -%} App landing page layout. Chains to default.html. IMPORTANT: this file's own `css: app` above only affects a page that has no `css:` of its own -- Jekyll does NOT cascade a layout's front matter down to the pages that use it. Every page on this layout must still set `css: app` itself (as cloud-storage-backup-drive.md does), or it renders with shell.css only and no pages/app.css. Confirmed the hard way: post.html originally relied on exactly this false cascade and pages/post.css silently never loaded. Fully front-matter-driven; the page body is empty (user, 29 Sep: no pasted guide text -- each app page is its own designed page). Required front matter (STATUS.md section 4): title, description, css (must be "app"), app_name, tagline, ios_url and/or android_url, category, features (icon, title, text), how_it_works (3x {title, text}), faqs (q, a). Optional, each adding a section: moments (+ moments_heading, moments_sub; 4x {icon, title, text}), compare ({heading, without[], with[], optional without_title / with_title}), tips (+ tips_heading; 3x {title, text}), guides (Daily Info slugs). Also steps_heading and trust_text (an app-specific privacy sentence -- only ever something confirmed about that particular app). Everything written for an app must stay inside what's confirmed about it (STATUS.md section 1): moments describe situations, tips and FAQs give general advice; neither may imply a feature the app isn't known to have. The #download id must stay on the hero's store badges. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Schema: applicationCategory is one of schema.org's application types (all four apps are utilities), not the page's visible category label. The apps are free to download (owner, 3 Oct), so the Offer is price 0; currency is required by Google even for free apps. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _clean_url = page.url -%} {%- comment -%} This page's own apps.yml entry (for its glyph/tone tile), found by URL: /document-reader-pdf -> document-reader-pdf. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _slug = page.url | remove_first: "/" -%} {%- assign _app = site.data.apps | where: "slug", _slug | first -%} {%- assign _short = _app.short | default: page.app_name -%} {%- comment -%} Platforms come from which store links the page actually has -- never hardcoded, since most of the apps are Android-only. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.ios_url and page.android_url -%} {%- assign _os = "iOS, Android" -%}{%- assign _platforms = "iPhone and Android" -%} {%- elsif page.ios_url -%} {%- assign _os = "iOS" -%}{%- assign _platforms = "iPhone" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign _os = "Android" -%}{%- assign _platforms = "Android" -%} {%- endif -%} {% include faq-schema.html faqs=page.faqs %} {%- comment -%} Everything visible sits inside the app's tone scope (shell.css "App tones"): buttons, links, step numbers, icon chips and the hero all take this app's colour. The header and footer stay outside it, in brand blue. Layout (redesigned 3 Oct): a two-column hero (copy left, the app's tile with its features right), then sections with left-aligned headings like the homepage -- no uppercase labels over headings, and open layouts rather than a box around every item: moments, how it works, features (one panel, or the bento for 6+), with/without, tips, related guides, FAQ beside its heading, other apps, and a closing pair of cards (get the app, your privacy). Every section is named by its heading (aria-labelledby). {%- endcomment %}
