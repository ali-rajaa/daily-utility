--- layout: default css: legal --- {%- comment -%} Privacy/terms layout. Chains to default.html. Every page on this layout must set `css: legal` in its OWN front matter (this file's `css: legal` above does not cascade -- see app.html's header comment for why). Required front matter (STATUS.md section 4): title, description, css (must be "legal"), heading (the H1; title is the longer ), last_updated, sections (a list of {id, label}). Optional: lead (an intro paragraph under the H1). The body is prose written as .md (so kramdown runs); every body heading must carry kramdown's inline ID syntax, e.g. `## Data we collect {#data-we-collect}`, matching one of the `sections` entries below, so the table of contents generated here always links to a heading that actually exists -- a hand-typed TOC would drift the moment a heading was renamed and nobody updated the list separately. No hardcoded canonical: here -- legal pages use the same canonical logic as every other page (default.html).
