{%- comment -%} A phone, drawn in CSS (shell.css "Phone frame"): a dark frame, a small camera dot and a screen. Device-neutral on purpose (the apps run on iPhone and Android) and no maker's design or logo. The screen shows the app's real screenshot once there is one: set `screenshot:` on the app's entry in _data/apps.yml (a portrait PNG or JPEG from the app, saved in assets/screens/). Until then it shows a launch screen built from what the site already has: the app's colour, its glyph and its name. Never a made-up interface. Params: app (an apps.yml entry), class (optional, sets the size via --phone-w).
