{%- comment -%} Decorative gradient-mesh glow for a hero section. Pure atmosphere: sits behind the content (z-index below it), aria-hidden, pointer-events:none. Uses --brand/--brand-2 (sampled from the real logo, see STATUS.md 4e) rather than the flat "vivid background" the Aurora-UI style calls for, since this site's brand colours already do that job. The drift animation is ~18s per cycle -- well clear of the ~5s (0.2Hz) range Apple's own motion guidance flags as uncomfortable -- and freezes under prefers-reduced-motion rather than just slowing down. Usage: {% include hero-glow.html %} as the first child of a position:relative container; pair with .hero-glow-host on that container (shell.css). {%- endcomment -%}
