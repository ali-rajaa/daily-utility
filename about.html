How we work

  • One job per app

    Backing up, reading documents, moving files and getting deleted photos back are different jobs, so each has its own app. That keeps every app simple to use.

  • Free to download

    All four apps are free to download from Google Play, and Cloud Storage Backup & Drive from the App Store as well.

  • Your files aren't sold

    We don't sell your private files, photos, videos, documents or contacts, and we only use them to provide the features you ask for. The privacy policy covers every app and this website.

  • Guides for everyone

    Daily Info is our free library of plain-English guides to everyday phone problems. Each guide is useful whether or not you use our apps.
