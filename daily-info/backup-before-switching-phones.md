Switching phones is one of the easiest times to lose something you didn't mean to: a photo album, a saved contact, a document you were partway through. A little preparation beforehand means the move is uneventful instead of stressful.

## Step 1: Start with your photos and videos

Your camera roll is usually the largest and most irreplaceable thing on your phone. Before you do anything else, confirm it's backed up somewhere other than the phone itself -- a cloud storage account, not just a memory card that could be lost in the same move.

## Step 2: Export your contacts

Contacts are easy to overlook because they quietly sync in the background for many people, but it's worth checking explicitly rather than assuming. Look in your phone's account settings for a contacts sync option, and confirm the last sync date isn't stale.

## Step 3: Check app data, not just app photos

Some apps keep data locally on the device instead of in the cloud -- notes, saved logins, offline documents. If an app matters to you, check its own settings for an export or backup option before you set the old phone aside.

## Step 4: Back up before you factory reset, not after

If you plan to wipe the old phone before trading it in or handing it down, always confirm the new phone has everything it needs *first*. Once a factory reset runs, anything that wasn't backed up is gone.

## Step 5: Restore in the same order

When you set up the new phone, restore photos and contacts first, then reinstall the apps you use most. That way, if anything looks off, you'll notice it early rather than weeks later when the old phone is already gone.

Automatic backup tools exist specifically to make this a non-event: rather than remembering to do all of the above manually before every phone change, an app that continuously backs up your photos, videos, contacts and app data means the "before I switch phones" checklist above is already done by the time you need it.
