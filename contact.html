What to put in your email

A few details up front mean fewer emails back and forth.

  1. Which app

    The app's name, if you didn't start from one of the links above.

  2. Your phone

    Its make and model, and whether it runs iOS or Android, with the version if you know it.

  3. What happened

    What you were doing, what you expected and what you saw instead. A screenshot often says it best.

Please don't include your password. Support doesn't need it to help you.
